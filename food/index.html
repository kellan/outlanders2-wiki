How much each source feeds

Food units a day from one full field or one tree, read as villagers fed, using the stored yields, growth days and fruit intervals and the 0.67 food units a villager burns a day. Processed foods (bread, cookies, cheese) depend on factory speed, which is not stored, so they are not ranked here. Real figures are lower: fruit waits to be picked, fields idle between harvests, and rich foods eaten early are partly wasted.
