MECHANICS

Happiness

How the needs a level switches on add up to happiness, what each met need gives back, and the levels that set a happiness target.

Weights

Each need counts towards happiness with a stored weight. Food counts most, then Heat, Cooling and Water, then Fun, Community and Wellness, and Housing least. A need only counts on levels that switch it on.

A typical level switches on Food, Housing, Community, Fun and Wellness. The share column assumes happiness is the weighted average of the needs that are met. That is a reading of the stored weights; the formula itself is code and has not been decoded.

What a met need gives back

A villager whose need is met gets a bonus on top of not suffering. Heat, Cooling and Water make every other meter fall 20% more slowly; the game calls this the Boost. A warm, cool or watered village needs less of everything else. Fun makes villagers walk faster, Food lets them carry faster, Housing shortens sleep, Community raises the chance of babies, and Wellness adds 0.2 days of life for every day it is met.

Where happiness matters

Births. Happiness raises the chance of a baby by up to 40% (stored multiplier 0.4). See Villagers and life.
