PLANT

Palm Tree

Palm Tree

Type
Fruit tree
Footprint
2 × 2
Yields
1 Fruit every 1.3–2 days, at most 5 waiting to be picked
Also
1 Log per tree when felled (3 chops)
Rate
about 0.61 Fruit a day while picked; a full tree holds 5 and fills in about 8 days
Planted by
Orchard
Harvested by
Foragers' Hut, Nopalero's Hut or Oasis
Biomes
Tropiclands
Grows wild
Tropiclands (0.02 per tile)
Spreads
2% chance a day to seed a new tree within 3 grid units, never closer than 1 grid unit to another plant
Feeds
about 0.91 villagers from one tree (0.61 Fruit a day = 0.61 food units a day; a villager eats 0.67)
Grows in
13 days (4 stages)
Levels
49 levels
