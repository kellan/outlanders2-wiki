For the first game

Outlanders 2 is an Apple Arcade game and is not on Steam; the Steam pages and older guides are for the first Outlanders. Many buildings, needs and leaders changed in the sequel, so treat their advice as background.

  • Outlanders discussions on Steam Steam not yet reviewed: Steam only has the first game. Outlanders 2 is not on Steam, and the board's sequel thread is players asking for a Steam release. Useful for the first game; advice there may not hold for the sequel.
  • Gamezebo: Outlanders guide, tips and strategies Gamezebo not yet reviewed: A written guide to the first game. Many buildings and mechanics changed in the sequel.

What belongs here

Anything that helps someone play: level walkthroughs, build-order videos, calculators, active forums. Not copies of this wiki, paywalled pages, or sites that exist to carry adverts. A maintainer checks each suggestion once and marks it listed; the link checker revisits every URL weekly and a dead link is removed.
