What belongs here

Anything that helps someone play: level walkthroughs, build-order videos, calculators, active forums. Not copies of this wiki, paywalled pages, or sites that exist to carry adverts. A maintainer checks each suggestion once and marks it listed; the link checker revisits every URL weekly and a dead link is removed.
