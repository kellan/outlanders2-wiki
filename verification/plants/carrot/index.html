Growth stages

The sapling definition stores 4 stages totalling 9 days: 2.25 + 2.25 + 2.25 + 2.25. sum of configured stages; regrowth, season effects, overrides and final-stage semantics not verified Nobody has timed this plant in play, so whether a stored day equals a clock day, and whether the last stage is the harvestable state or a wait before it, are open questions.

Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

  • Growth-only average per fully planted field; planting, harvesting, walking and replanting add time.
  • More workers can tend more fields or reduce delays; they do not shorten the configured growth stages.
