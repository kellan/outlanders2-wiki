Contributed observations

Drinking one mug of Beer takes a villager's Fun meter from low to completely full in one step, with no half-full stage. Seen twice: a brewer on day 30, and on day 44 a villager whose activity read "Drinking a mug of Beer" while her Fun was still low. That is more than the Fun 2 (50 points) the field guide shows and fits the stored Fun 4 (100 points).

Status: confirmed · gameplay-test · 30 (254)

Local agent playtest, 27 October 2026: wiki branch codex/playtest-2026-10-27 (commits f92548fd and 869d2440), docs/INFORMATION-ANSWERS.md there, entries "Beer — low-to-full Fun refill captured on day 30" and "Beer — drinking action linked to a low-to-full Fun refill"; issue 10 comments 6448914817 and 6450897175. Screenshots: gameplay-evidence/beer/ (brewery-guide, day30-joshua-before-172609530Z, day30-joshua-after-172609916Z, day30-paused-after-fruit, day44-violet-drinking, day44-violet-low-during-drink, day44-violet-before, day44-violet-after, day44-violet-after-action).

Trade sandbox, Evergreen, 10 villagers, the Brewery the only Fun source and no Fun gear. Day 30: Joshua's ring went from low to full between captures 0.4 s apart, but his action was not visible. Day 44: Violet's action read "Drinking a mug of Beer" with Fun still low; on resuming, it went full between frames at 18:54:17.672 and 18:54:17.843 UTC, then her action read "Having a sudden rush of inspiration". The ring has no numbers and a minimum coloured segment, so the start point is approximate and an exact +100 is not measured; nobody saw the Beer stock fall by one. Cookie and Stew, which also show different values, are untested.
