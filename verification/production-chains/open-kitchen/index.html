◌
Material balance, not production speed

These ratios eliminate intermediate states. Worker availability, equipment, storage, hauling and animation timing still need gameplay checks.

◌
Recipe discrepancy

The decoded steps burn 1 Stick into 5 fires and use one fire per cook, so the worked-out ratio is 1/5 Stick per 3 Stew; the field guide counts the whole Stick. Stew's own stored values restore Food 1 and no Community: the Community 2 the guide shows is its display value (ResourceBehaviour.Visual), and the Community a villager gets comes from eating at the Open Kitchen's social spots (4 units a visit).

◌
Some branches need interpretation
