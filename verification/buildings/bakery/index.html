Gameplay facts and their evidence

Recipe per cycle (confirmed): 1 Flour + 1 Stick → 8 Bread. field-guide, 2026-10-24: Bakery field guide screenshot (gameplay-evidence/bakery-field-guide.png). Maintainer play report of 25 October 2026 agrees. The decoded formula graph gives exactly 8 Bread per Flour; formula 297381 is named "1 Stick -> 8 KitchenFire" although its serialized output is 10, so whether leftover fire carries between cycles is unverified.

Output capacity (open): 60 Bread. decoded-data, 2026-10-26: Four public Bread piles (ResourcePileBehaviour 277583, 287164, 311412, 313966) each store Capacity 15 (research handoff, issue 10 comment 6405318044). The maintainer withdrew an earlier estimate of 40 on 26 October and asked that the decoded value be used. Whether all four piles are active and how the stop condition works is still to be traced.
