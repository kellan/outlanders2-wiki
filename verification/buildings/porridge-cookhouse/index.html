Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds and this building works 60% of it. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

No daily estimate: Separate supply of BarleyPorridgeEmptyPlate; No resolved timer for BarleyReady -> Barley.

Contributed observations

The small Community rise from Barley Porridge comes from eating at the Porridge Cookhouse: its eating spot stores Community 1, while the dish stores none.

Status: confirmed · decoded-data · 30 (254)

SocialSpotBehaviour 277041 on the Porridge Cookhouse stores CommunityUnits 1 with Duration 6 (research handoff, issue 10 comment 6405320280; SocialEatingPlaceBehaviour 314244). The Barley Porridge resource stores CommunityUnits 0. Open Kitchen eating spot 276989 stores Community 4, Desert Cookhouse 276991 Fun 4, Fondue Bar 292958 Fun 4.

Stored value confirmed and matches the maintainer’s “small amount”. How a stored unit converts to the need meter, and whether the dish gives anything when eaten elsewhere, still need the off-site test.
