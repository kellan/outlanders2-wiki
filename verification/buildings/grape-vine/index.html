Growth stages

The sapling definition stores 4 stages totalling 13 days: 3.25 + 3.25 + 3.25 + 3.25. sum of configured stages; regrowth, season effects, overrides and final-stage semantics not verified Nobody has timed this plant in play, so whether a stored day equals a clock day, and whether the last stage is the harvestable state or a wait before it, are open questions.
