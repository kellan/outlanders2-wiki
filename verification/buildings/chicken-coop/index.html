Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds and this building works 60% of it. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

No daily estimate: Separate supply of Egg.

  • Player-reported maximum: 5 eggs per game day per coop. The building owns its chickens. This total has not yet been independently reproduced from the animal laying logic.
  • Collection takes about 5.3 simulation seconds per egg; cleaning also uses worker time. Neither collection speed nor the number of chickens alone establishes the laying rate.

Contributed observations

A Chicken Coop produces up to 5 Eggs per game day regardless of how many workers are assigned; extra workers collect and clean.

Status: open · player-report · 30 (254)

Maintainer report relayed in the research handoff, issue 10 comment 6405316937. Collection formula 282694 takes about 5.3 simulation seconds per egg; adult chicken config 303909 stores an extraction interval of 60.

Not independently reproduced from the laying logic; the two MaxSpawned=5 spawners under the coop spawn manure, not eggs.
