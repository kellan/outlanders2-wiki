◌
Recipe discrepancy

The guide lists both goods under one Wheat count. The building's outputs are exclusive and the game files store two separate 12 Wheat trades, so one trade gives 6 Glass Panes or 6 Animal Hides, whichever is selected on the Resources tab. Corrected 2026-10-26; this page first read it as both.

Sum of distinct material formulas in the linked construction stages. Repeated stages and alternatives need gameplay verification.

This exact definition appears in the stored build/plant lists below. Runtime unlocks and menu visibility remain untested.

Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds and this building works 60% of it. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

Gameplay facts and their evidence

Recipe per cycle (confirmed): 8 Glass Pane → 6 Animal Hide or 9 Wheat. field-guide, 2026-10-27: Field guide screenshot gameplay-evidence/trade/tropiclands-outpost-guide.jpg (Local agent playtest, 27 October 2026: wiki branch codex/playtest-2026-10-27 (commits 95534692 and f272b4b5), docs/INFORMATION-ANSWERS.md there, entry "Tropiclands Trading Outpost — full field-guide card"). The guide shows the input and both imports under one card; as for the Evergreen outpost, the outputs are exclusive in the prefab and the stored trades match, so a trade gives one import or the other. No trade was run in this biome.
