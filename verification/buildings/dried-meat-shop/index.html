Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds and this building works 60% of it. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

No daily estimate: No resolved timer for Sliced Meat -> Drying Meat.
