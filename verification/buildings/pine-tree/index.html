Piles

Every pile this building prefab defines, from the decoded ResourcePileBehaviour records. Public output piles are what the stockpile and other buildings can take from; the rest hold work in progress. The game stops a workspot when its output piles are full; which piles count as full for a given step is not stored here.

Growth stages

The sapling definition stores 4 stages totalling 10 days: 2.5 + 2.5 + 2.5 + 2.5. sum of configured stages; regrowth, season effects, overrides and final-stage semantics not verified Nobody has timed this plant in play, so whether a stored day equals a clock day, and whether the last stage is the harvestable state or a wait before it, are open questions.

Modelled production capacity

A game day is 300 simulation seconds. The model assumes all inputs delivered and output cleared, and excludes walking, handoffs and contention; it is a ceiling for comparison, not a measured yield.

  • Growth time is initial establishment. Felling yield is separate from repeat fruit production.
