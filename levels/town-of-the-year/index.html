Town of the Year

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Chill · batch 17, 20 of 27 · released 26 Mar 2025 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

Life has become so good in the beautiful small town on the shores of Pison's river, the villagers entered the Town of the Year contest. Now they’re stuck chasing bigger crowds and happier faces.

  • Town of the Year is a Chill challenge with an 80-day limit: population 100 and satisfy Happiness, with everyone settled as the optional objective. Chill does not mean unlimited days. tested in play, 2026-10-26

    Matches the stored 80-day limit for this map. The in-game Challenges screen describes Redux as familiar levels with a twist, Bounty as producing or stocking as much of a resource as possible within a time limit, and Original as new handcrafted campaign-like levels; ranked Original challenges are timed in days.
