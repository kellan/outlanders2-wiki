Breadification Bias

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

An eccentric but well-meaning group of researchers is venturing to the coldest ends of the Outlands to conduct an experiment testing their theory that a diet based on bread and stew leads to an increase in population. If they tweak the results just a little, they might finally get published. The 45th time's the charm!
