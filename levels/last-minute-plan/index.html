Last Minute Plan

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Ranked · batch 10, 13 of 27 · released 18 Dec 2024 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

← Cordial Work EnvironmentBundle Up! →

Dark, stormy clouds loom on the horizon. A blizzard is approaching, and this small town in the Winterlands faces a harsh reality: there won’t be enough food to survive if it hits. The plan is simple—build a second greenhouse as quickly as possible.
