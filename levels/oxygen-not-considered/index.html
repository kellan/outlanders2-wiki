Oxygen not Considered

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Ranked · batch 16, 19 of 27 · released 12 Mar 2025 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

← Grain of SunMagnus Opus →

Preparations for the year-end concert on the mainland screeched to a halt when organizers ran out of wood for the main stage. Desperate to stay on schedule, workers rushed to a nearby archipelago to gather as many logs as they could.
