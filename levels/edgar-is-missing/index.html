Edgar is Missing

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Things have gone south in the islands since Edgar left. The joy of witnessing his self-worth journey was exhilarating for the villagers, and now, with the arrival of winter, snow, and winds, they take this opportunity for self-reflection, to cozy up and enjoy warm moments at home with loved ones and good meals.
