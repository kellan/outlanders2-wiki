    Strategy Community · draft

    The opening level only asks for the two gathering huts and a small log stockpile, so the fastest finish is to build both huts immediately next to the trees and let the villagers stock logs.

    Suggested build order

    1. Foragers' Hut First objective; place it next to bushes so foragers keep the five adults fed.
    2. Lumberjacks' Base Second objective; the six logs it stocks complete the third.

    Watch out for

    • The optional objective is to recycle the Canoe; it is easy to forget once the main objectives are done.
