Port Plateau

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

The rapid onset of extreme temperatures vaporized the water around a small island, turning it into a plateau and leaving its villagers in shock. With no end in sight, they can never be fully prepared.

How the totals were worked out
  • 14 Canteen at the Canteen Factory needs 28 Glass Pane + 5.6 Stick + 14 Reed Bunch
  • 28 Glass Pane at the Glassworks needs 42 Stick
  • 42 Stick at the Firewood Yard needs 10.5 Log
  • 5.6 Stick at the Firewood Yard needs 1.4 Log

Construction materials for the buildings themselves are not included yet. Recipe ratios come from each resource's stored recipe; see the resource pages for their evidence.
