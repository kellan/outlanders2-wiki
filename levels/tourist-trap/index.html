Tourist Trap

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Chill · batch 06, 8 of 27 · released 9 Oct 2024 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

← Bashful ValleyMorrigwyn Stones →

Twice a year, on a treacherous island at the northernmost edge of the Outlands, the Trapper's Guild hosts an event where amateur trappers test their skills over a few intense days. The island is known as 'The Vortex,' a name coined by the guild to add an air of mystery and excitement to the event.

What the objectives need

Items the objectives ask for, plus the ingredients to make them, worked back through each recipe using the buildings this map offers. Amounts are rounded up to whole items; allow extra for anything villagers use along the way. Items that can be picked up or harvested from the map stop there rather than being worked back through a factory.
