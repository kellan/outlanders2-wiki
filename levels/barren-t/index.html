Barren't

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

According to 'Myths and Lore of the Outlands Vol. IV', this lush jungle oasis is artificial, created long ago by one of the most adventurous among the legendary 'first outlanders'. Villagers love to explore the jungle's secrets and share their discoveries over drinks at night.

How the totals were worked out
  • 10 Canteen at the Canteen Factory needs 20 Glass Pane + 4 Stick + 10 Reed Bunch
  • 20 Glass Pane at the Glassworks needs 30 Stick
  • 30 Stick at the Firewood Yard needs 7.5 Log
  • 4 Stick at the Firewood Yard needs 1 Log
