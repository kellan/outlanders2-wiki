Challenge levels

Each weekend there is an Original; on weekdays Bounty and Redux alternate. Chill, Ranked and Remix are older challenges that are still playable from the archive but are no longer in the rotation. See Challenges.

Original in the rotation

A new level every weekend, Friday to Sunday. These levels are downloaded when they run and are not in the installed game files, so the wiki does not have their maps yet. Seen in the archive so far: Counterspell.

Redux in the rotation

A simpler or changed version of an earlier level. Weekdays, 24 hours each, alternating with Bounty. These levels are downloaded when they run and are not in the installed game files, so the wiki does not have their maps yet. Seen in the archive so far: Friends in the Frost Redux (a Redux of Friends in the Frost).

Bounty in the rotation

Make or hold as much of one resource as you can. Weekdays, 24 hours each, alternating with Redux. These levels are downloaded when they run and are not in the installed game files, so the wiki does not have their maps yet. Seen in the archive so far: Mysterious Gingerbread Man Seminar.
