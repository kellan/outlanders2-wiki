Diablo Shores

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Ah, the golden sandy beaches of Diablo Shores! A quiet, paradisiac corner of the Outlands, flying under the radar of most... until now. Word has spread, and people are flocking in, turning it into a full-fledged summer beach town like any other. So it goes.

What the objectives need

Items the objectives ask for, plus the ingredients to make them, worked back through each recipe using the buildings this map offers. Amounts are ratios: round up to whole items and allow extra for anything villagers use along the way.
