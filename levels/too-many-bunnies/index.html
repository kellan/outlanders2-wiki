Too Many Bunnies

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Remix · batch 01, 2 of 27 · released 17 Jul 2024 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

← Barren'tRoad Snow →

With their leader gone and winter in full swing, the villagers at the Outlands' cookie capital face their biggest hurdle yet: wild animals have devoured all of their cookies. Unable to bake more until spring, they're gonna need to make a drastic change to their diet.

What the objectives need

Items the objectives ask for, plus the ingredients to make them, worked back through each recipe using the buildings this map offers. Amounts are rounded up to whole items; allow extra for anything villagers use along the way. Items that can be picked up or harvested from the map stop there rather than being worked back through a factory.
