Eternal Summer

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Challenges › Chill · batch 04, 5 of 27 · released 28 Aug 2024 · an older challenge, still playable from the archive but no longer in the rotation

← The RekindlingCliffside Resort →

As kids, the gang would swim to Peak Island every summer, daydreaming of building their own town there. When the time came to turn this dream into reality, the beautiful terrain they once idealized proved a little difficult to build on.

ObjectiveWhat counts
Have 4 Brick Houses.Have this many standing at once.

Objectives are worded as the game shows them: the level’s own objective text where it has one, otherwise the game’s standard objective lines.

What the objectives need

Items the objectives ask for, plus the ingredients to make them, worked back through each recipe using the buildings this map offers. Amounts are rounded up to whole items; allow extra for anything villagers use along the way. Items that can be picked up or harvested from the map stop there rather than being worked back through a factory.
