Babies and births

How often babies are born, what speeds it up or stops it, and the maps where no babies are born at all.

How a birth happens

  • Once a game day, if the village has more than one adult, every house that has residents and an empty crib rolls once for a baby.
  • Each house has one crib (the Shelter House has none). A baby lives in its crib until it turns 3 and becomes a kid, then moves into a bed. So a house can have one baby at a time.
  • A baby is not a villager yet. It does not count toward the population or a population goal until it becomes a kid, 3 days after birth.
  • Births stop at 200 villagers.

The chance, step by step

Each roll starts at 14% and is multiplied by:

What that means in babies a day

With 10 houses whose cribs start empty. After a birth the crib is busy for 3 days, so a house averages one baby every (1 ÷ chance + 3) days.

  • Houses are the lever. Each house adds its own roll, so babies a day grows with the number of houses with residents.
  • Keep a spare bed. A village with every bed taken has only 2.5% of its usual chance.
  • Happiness and Community help, but less than more houses do: together they are worth about 1.68 times the base chance.
  • The tournament grow-up boost makes children age twice as fast, so cribs free up in about 1.5 days.

Rates assume the factors hold steady and every house has residents; real villages swing with happiness and beds. The roll is random, so short runs vary a lot.

Maps where no babies are born

On 15 maps a map rule sets the birth chance to zero for the whole level. The population only grows from villagers who arrive, so plan houses, workers and any population goal around the villagers you have.

Story and standalone maps: Alike!, Full Tilt Queso, An Apple a Day, Unidentified, The End of the World and Middle Ground.

Online challenges: Retirement Town, "The Rekindling" Redux, Summer Camp, "Alike!" Redux, "An Apple a Day" Redux, "Summer Camp" Redux, Overgrown, Company Picnic and Mother Knows Best.

On The End of the World the same rule also stops deaths and keeps happiness fixed.

Confirmed in play on Full Tilt Queso.

Found from the level events placed on each map: “No Reproduction”, “Level 01A” and “Level 01B” set the birth multiplier to 0 for 999 days from day 1. Each level page says so too.

Where this comes from

From the game code (Level.BirthBabies and ReproductionValues.GetBirthChance, version 30), the stored ReproductionValues (base 14%, happiness 0.4, full housing -0.975, children per adult -0.4, limit 200), the need effects and the tournament boosts. Nobody has timed birth rates in play yet.
