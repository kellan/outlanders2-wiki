Why villagers pick it

Villagers prefer eating Fondue at the Fondue Bar over food with the same 2-unit value such as Bread or Dried Meat: the meal also gives Fun 4 for eating in company, and the game ranks eating at a gathering place a little above eating alone (see how eating works). The food value itself is the same.

How it is made

Fondue Bar

Recipe: 1 Cheese + 1 Stick + 2 Veggie → 5 Fondue (Fondue Empty Plate reused)

Each Fondue: 1 Cheese for every 5 Fondue, 1 Stick for every 5 Fondue, 1 Veggie for every 2.5 Fondue

Smallest whole batch: 1 Cheese + 1 Stick + 2 Veggie → 5 Fondue

Read from the stored steps: alternatives shown as “any of”, fuel folded into fractions, containers that come back from the eating or drinking spots marked reused. Not timed in play.
