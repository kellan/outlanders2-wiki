How it is made

Nopalero's Hut

Recipe: 1 Nopalito (from the map) → 1 Nopalito

Each Nopalito: 1 Nopalito (from the map)

Alternatives are shown as “any of”, fuel is folded into fractions, and containers that come back from eating or drinking spots are marked reused.

Oasis

Recipe: 1 Nopalito (from the map) → 1 Nopalito

Each Nopalito: 1 Nopalito (from the map)

Alternatives are shown as “any of”, fuel is folded into fractions, and containers that come back from eating or drinking spots are marked reused.

Also produced by Nopal Tree (recipe not reduced to a single ratio).
