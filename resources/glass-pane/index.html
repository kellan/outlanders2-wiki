How it is made

Trading Outpost (Evergreen)

Each Glass Pane: 2 Wheat

Per trade: 12 Wheat → 6 Glass Pane or 6 Animal Hide (field guide)

Trading Outpost (Winterlands)

Each Glass Pane: 1.333 Animal Hide

Per trade: 8 Animal Hide → 9 Wheat or 6 Glass Pane (field guide)

Glassworks

Each Glass Pane: 1.5 Stick

Smallest whole batch: 3 Stick → 2 Glass Pane
