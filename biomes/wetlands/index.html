BIOME

Wetlands

Released · maintainer report

Game data v32 · build 257Sources & research notes

Needs & environment

See individual scenarios for their enabled needs.

Sandbox build list

What a new sandbox game in this biome lets you plant and build, as stored in the game files. Not yet checked in play.

Shared by every biome (the game’s base list): Advanced Warehouse, Brick Road, Brickyard, Bridge, Clay Pit, Communal Plaza, Firewood Yard, Fitness Park, Foresters' Hub, Lumberjacks' Base, Market, Paper Mill, Playground, Press, Road, Roadworks Office, Sawmill, Stone Mine, Stone Road, Warehouse, Wood Statue, Zen Garden.

Terrain: No rock source is scattered and none of Oona’s three maps has mountain tiles, so there is no Stone here. Much of the ground is shallow marsh (stored as Sand tiles), where, as the patch notes say, villagers sink in and walk slower: ×0.7 in the version 32 code, and they route around it (path cost 1.5 instead of 1.1). See Roads and walking.

Plants

  • Field crops: Corn, Tomato, Pumpkin (planted by the Farm)
  • Fruit trees: Berry Bush, Grape Vine (planted by the Orchard)
  • Forestry: Willow Tree, Cypress Tree (planted by the Foresters' Hub)

Houses

Basic House, Brick House, Plank House, Two-Story House

Workplaces

Beehive, Book Workshop, Bootmakers' Shop, Cheesery, Concoction Shop, Dried Meat Shop, Farm, Fishing Dock, Fondue Bar, Foragers' Hut, Luthierie, Mead Hall, Open Kitchen, Orchard, Smoothie Stand, Stroganoff Cookhouse, Swamp Trappers' Hut

Gathering places & decoration

Calla Lilly Planter, Fountain, Hibiscus Planter, Jacaranda Tree Pit, Orchid Tree Pit

Compared with other biomes

Not offered here, though other biomes have it. Drylands, Evergreen, Tropiclands, Winterlands and Spookylands all offer Quarry. Evergreen, Tropiclands, Winterlands and Spookylands all offer Bakery, Brewery, Chicken Coop, Cookie Shop, Pendant Craftshop, Trading Outpost and Windmill. Evergreen and Spookylands offer Bottle Factory, Porridge Cookhouse and Winery. Only here: Concoction Shop, Stroganoff Cookhouse, Swamp Trappers' Hut.
