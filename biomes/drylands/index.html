BIOME

Drylands

Game data v31 · build 255Sources & research notes

Needs & environment

The conditions of the Drylands are a challenge for outlanders. Being dehydrated for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Sandbox build list

What a new sandbox game in this biome lets you plant and build, as stored in the game files. Buildings every biome shares, such as the Market, Warehouse, Sawmill and roads, are not stored per biome and are not listed; they are presumed available everywhere. Not yet checked in play.

Plants

  • Field crops: Watermelon, Carrot (planted by the Oasis)
  • Fruit trees: Date Palm Tree, Nopal Tree (planted by the Oasis)
  • Forestry: Mesquite Tree (no planter in this list)

Houses

Desert Home

Workplaces

Desert Cookhouse, Nopalero's Hut, Oasis, Quarry, Smoothie Stand, Well

Compared with other biomes

Not offered here, though other biomes have it. Evergreen, Tropiclands, Winterlands and Spookylands all offer Bakery, Basic House, Brick House, Chicken Coop, Cookie Shop, Fountain, Luthierie, Open Kitchen, Plank House, Two-Story House and Windmill. Evergreen, Tropiclands and Winterlands all offer Book Workshop, Bootmakers' Shop, Brewery, Dried Meat Shop, Fishing Dock, Foresters' Hub, Pendant Craftshop and Trading Outpost. Evergreen, Tropiclands and Spookylands all offer Farm, Foragers' Hut and Orchard. Evergreen, Winterlands and Spookylands all offer Cheesery and Fondue Bar. Evergreen and Spookylands offer Beehive, Calla Lilly Planter, Jacaranda Tree Pit, Mead Hall and Porridge Cookhouse. Only here: Desert Cookhouse, Desert Home, Nopalero's Hut, Oasis, Well.

Still offered on hand-made maps in this biome: Basic House ("Seventeen" Redux); Brick House (Spicy Nopalito Shindig); Calla Lilly Planter (Mythical Pendant Fantasy); Dried Meat Shop ("Seventeen" Redux); Foresters' Hub (Bewildering Water Fiesta, Familiar, Opulent Manchamanteles Extravaganza, Outgrown and 9 more); Fountain (Mythical Pendant Fantasy, Outlandish Ukulele Gala, Secret Oasis, Spicy Nopalito Shindig); Luthierie (Outlandish Ukulele Gala, Secret Oasis); Open Kitchen (Outlandish Ukulele Gala); Orchard (Familiar, Outgrown, Secret Oasis, "Seventeen" Redux and 4 more); Pendant Craftshop (Mythical Pendant Fantasy, Secret Oasis, "Summer Camp" Redux); Plank House ("Seventeen" Redux).
