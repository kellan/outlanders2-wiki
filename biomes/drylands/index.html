BIOME

Drylands

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Needs & environment

The conditions of the Drylands are a challenge for outlanders. Being dehydrated for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Plants & buildings

Available plants and buildings depend on the scenario. Each scenario page lists its own planting and building options.
