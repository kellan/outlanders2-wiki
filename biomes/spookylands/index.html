Spookylands

Game data v31 · build 255Sources & research notes

Needs & environment

See individual scenarios for their enabled needs.

Sandbox build list

What a new sandbox game in this biome lets you plant and build, as stored in the game files. Buildings every biome shares, such as the Market, Warehouse, Sawmill and roads, are not stored per biome and are not listed; they are presumed available everywhere. Not yet checked in play.

Plants

  • Field crops: Wheat, Pumpkin, Corn, Cabbage, Eggplant (planted by the Farm)
  • Fruit trees: Fig Tree, Apple Tree, Berry Bush (planted by the Orchard)
  • Forestry: Dead Tree (no planter in this list)

Houses

Basic House, Brick House, Plank House, Two-Story House

Workplaces

Bakery, Beehive, Cheesery, Chicken Coop, Cookie Shop, Farm, Fondue Bar, Foragers' Hut, Luthierie, Mead Hall, Open Kitchen, Orchard, Porridge Cookhouse, Quarry, Smoothie Stand, Windmill

Gathering places & decoration

Calla Lilly Planter, Communal Plaza, Fountain, Halloween Tombstone, Jacaranda Tree Pit, Playground, Rose Planter, Zen Garden

Compared with other biomes

Not offered here, though other biomes have it. Evergreen, Tropiclands and Winterlands all offer Book Workshop, Bootmakers' Shop, Brewery, Dried Meat Shop, Fishing Dock, Foresters' Hub, Pendant Craftshop and Trading Outpost. Only here: Halloween Tombstone, Rose Planter. The shared Playground, Plaza and Zen Garden are excluded here, swapped for this biome’s own versions in the list above.

Still offered on hand-made maps in this biome: Book Workshop ("Drifting Docks" Redux, "Friends in the Frost" Redux); Bootmakers' Shop ("Drifting Docks" Redux, "Friends in the Frost" Redux, Mysterious Gingerbread Man Seminar); Brewery ("Drifting Docks" Redux, "Friends in the Frost" Redux, Morrigwyn Stones, Mysterious Gingerbread Man Seminar and 1 more); Dried Meat Shop ("Drifting Docks" Redux, "Save the Forests" Redux); Fishing Dock (Dark Stew Feast, "Drifting Docks" Redux, "Save the Forests" Redux, Spooktown); Foresters' Hub (A Moon Part, Dark Stew Feast, "Friends in the Frost" Redux, Morrigwyn Stones and 3 more); Trading Outpost (A Moon Part, "Drifting Docks" Redux, "Friends in the Frost" Redux).

Seen in play

  • The Halloween event fills the map with heavy mist: blue-grey at night, a pinkish haze by day. tested in play, 2026-11-08

    Matches the game data: the "Halloween Event" level event lasts 9,999 days and holds one particle system and no gameplay effect (every multiplier is 1). It is placed on The Great Pumpkin Race, Morrigwyn Stones and A Moon Part.

  • On Halloween maps the candles at the gravestones burn with green flames, and small green lights drift among the trees at night. tested in play, 2026-11-08

    The map places 37 Halloween candles and three Fireflies events; which effect makes the drifting green lights is not settled from the files.
