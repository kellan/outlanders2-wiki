BIOME

Winterlands

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

In-game field guide

  • Greenhouse field guide: enables farmers to plant fields and harvest vegetables in the Winterland biome.
  • Listed fields: Potato, Beet, Cabbage.
  • Construction: 6 Glass Panes + 6 Stone. Worker slots: 3.
View field-guide screenshot

Needs & environment

Extreme low temperatures in the Winterlands make survival tough. Being cold for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Plants & buildings

Available plants and buildings depend on the scenario. Each scenario page lists its own planting and building options.
