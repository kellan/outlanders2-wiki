BIOME

Tropiclands

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Needs & environment

High temperatures in the Tropiclands make daytime work tough. Lack of Cooling will reduce a villager's movement speed. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Plants & buildings

Available plants and buildings depend on the scenario. Each scenario page lists its own planting and building options.
