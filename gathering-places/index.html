MECHANICS

Gathering places

The game says only that villagers “like to gather” at parks, plazas, kitchens and pubs. The files say what a visit is worth: each place has a number of spots, and each spot carries the Community, Fun, Wellness, Heat or Cooling units a villager gains from one use. These are stored settings; how a unit moves the meter and how often villagers drop by are not yet measured.

How to read it

Spots are how many villagers can use the place at once. One visit gives is what each villager gains per use; compare it with the meters on the Needs page, where Community falls 20 a day and one unit restores 25. Can be picked when seeking is a stored switch: a villager looking for Community or Fun may set out for the place. Eating spots are switched off for the need they themselves give (an Open Kitchen cannot be picked for Community, a Fondue Bar not for Fun): you go there to eat, and the bonus comes with the meal. Where a spot shows no units, the value sits elsewhere: the dish or drink carries the food units at pubs and cookhouses, and the Bonfire's warmth comes from its heat pile (see its Heating row).

Spots come in two moods: the files hold separate animations for using a spot alone and with company, and most spots name a neighbour. Community by its nature needs company, so a lone villager on a bench gains less than the table suggests; the exact rule is code.

A villager cannot reuse the same spot straight away: the stored delay is 0.5–0.7 of a day depending on the place.
