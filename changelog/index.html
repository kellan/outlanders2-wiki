WIKI & VERSION HISTORY

Changelog

Two lists: what changed on this wiki, newest first, and what changed in each game update as far as the wiki can establish it. The current data baseline is version 30, build 254.

Wiki changes

One line per merged change, with the page to look at. Kept in CHANGELOG.md; reasoning and provenance are in content-notes/ and, for older changes, CONTENT-NOTES.md.

Unreleased

  • Reader pages lose stray hedges and raw data: no more source-data dumps on the Challenges, Tournament and campaign pages; chain pages drop the stock "has not been established" lines; spoiler reasons are in plain words (85).
  • Downloaded challenge levels no longer carry a "Starting state not checked in play" tag. They are decoded the same way as the levels that ship with the game, which never had one (84).
  • Pages repeat themselves less: tables drop columns that never change, building, chain, resource and plant pages state each recipe and cost once, each mechanics explanation lives on one page with links from the rest, and the Spoilers, Verification and Tournament pages list things once (83).
  • Challenge pages are tidier: one table per challenge type listing each level’s objectives, no leftover example section, a shorter rotation explanation, and older challenge levels filed under Challenges (82).
  • All 260 Original, Redux and Bounty challenge levels are on the wiki: objectives, starting conditions and the dates each one runs, with a table on each challenge type page (81).
  • Tournament Mode lists every season’s 14 rewards with the Reward Points each needs, including the live Rural season, decoded from the reward lists packaged with the game (80).
  • Levels are easier to browse: the home page links each campaign and each of the six challenge types, every challenge type has its own page explaining what it is and when it runs, and All levels is one sortable table (79).
  • Site search: a search box in the top bar on every page, with results filtered by section (buildings, food, levels, mechanics and so on) (78).
  • Text is larger and easier to read, tables line up, and wide screens get more room (77).
  • Pages now carry their own search description, canonical address, link-preview image and structured data, and the site publishes a sitemap, robots.txt, an llms.txt guide for AI assistants and a proper 404 page (76).
  • Campaigns & levels now splits campaign levels from challenge levels, and groups the challenges by type: Original, Redux and Bounty in the current rotation, and the older Chill, Ranked and Remix sets with their release dates (every two weeks from July 2024 to March 2025). The Challenges page explains the rotation (74).
  • Icons are back to the earlier style; the game-composited set from #72 is withdrawn. The Buildings page subtitle links work (issue 62).
  • More playtest results: one mug of Beer refills a villager's Fun from low to full, which supports the stored Fun 4 over the 2 the field guide shows; 20 Fishing Dock casts at the 0–2 spot gave 1.35 Meat a cast, and a dock stops at 72 Meat; a Trading Outpost trade was no faster with three workers; and the Tropiclands and Winterlands outposts' field guides match the stored trades (issue 10).
  • The home page leads with the biome islands and gives every section a game icon, with small icons beside building, plant, resource and food links and portraits beside leaders (#70).
  • Seven more buildings are checked against the in-game field guide: the four houses, Playground, Zen Garden and Fitness Park. A finished Bakery's refund (5 of 9 Clay, 2 of 4 Stone) shows demolition gives back half of each material, rounded up. A timed Trading Outpost trade confirms you get one import at a time and puts a trade at about three quarters of a game day (#69, issues 10 and 31).
  • Footprints now count in-game squares: a crop field is 1 × 1, a fruit tree 2 × 2, a Basic House 2 × 2. Before, they showed the stored grid units, which are twice as fine. Distances whose unit isn't confirmed, such as tree seeding range, now say "grid units" instead of "tiles". The Food page adds food per square, where crops beat trees, and drops columns that said the same thing on every row. Plant pages also say whether a field is one plant (Potato: 3 Veggie) or several (Carrot: 3 plants, 1 each) (#68).
  • New Sandbox page in Mechanics: the three starting-population presets, the three map sizes, the map generator's 15 buildable tiles and 3 food sources per villager, and each biome's crops, fruit trees, forestry plants and extra need (#67, issue 29).
  • The demolition test now asks for a Bakery (9 Clay + 4 Stone) to check whether "half back" holds for other buildings and how odd amounts round; the Basic House result is already recorded (#66, issue 31).
  • The plants list loses its junk "Base" row, and Cypress Tree and Willow Tree move to Spoilers: the game files gate them to a future update. An audit found every other unlisted building is a real campaign building. zBeer Stand is now shown as Beer Stand (#65).
  • The Trading Outpost now shows each biome's trade as a choice: Evergreen gives 12 Wheat for 6 Glass Pane *or* 6 Animal Hide, not both as the page said before. A table compares the Tropiclands, Evergreen and Winterlands trades, a short explainer covers how a trade runs, and the unused base and test-only versions are hidden (#64).
  • Building, DairyFarm and Stock no longer have pages. They are internal or test-only prefabs that never appear in play (#63, issue 62).
  • Icons now show white glyphs with dark lines, as in the game, instead of letting the tile colour show through; the fill is computed from the game's own outlines until the game's fill layer is exported. The home page has Mechanics and Reference boxes alongside Buildings and Plants (#61).
  • The Needs page is now a short hub: one table of the eight needs, each linking to its own page (Food, Community and so on) with its meter, the items and places that restore it, and where it applies. Eating has its own page, How eating works. Old links to sections of the Needs page still land in the right place (#60).
  • Findings from the local agent's play session: field guides for 38 more buildings, all checked against the game files (the Trading Outpost trades 12 Wheat for 6 Glass Panes and 6 Animal Hides), what demolishing a Basic House gives back, the day counter turning at dawn, a five-day meal log on Needs, the Drylands and Winterlands planting menus, the Rural season rewards, and confirmed campaign level order. Pages now show a "Seen in play" section (#59).
  • Guides elsewhere gains what a web search turned up: the Outlanders 2 Fandom wiki, YouTube level walkthroughs, Apple's launch notes and the App Store listing. Links for the first game, including the Steam discussions (Outlanders 2 is not on Steam), sit in their own section. All are marked not yet reviewed (#58, issue 38).
  • The whole site now uses the in-game field guide's look: the game's stored colour palette and its interface font, Bricolage Grotesque; a dark mode that follows your system, with a toggle in the top bar; icons on tiles in their game category colour; and a field-guide card on every building page laid out like the game's: each icon in its own colour, any-of inputs under one bracket, and Provides with the need values the guide shows. Table sorting now works on the published pages (#57, issue 56).
  • A Mechanics section replaces the Needs link in the menu: new pages for Happiness (weights, the Boost, levels with a target), Villagers and life (ages, births, days to starve or freeze), Heat, cooling and water (heat sources, Tropical weather), Days and time (a game day is five minutes, shifts, day limits) and Gear, with Needs, Houses and Gathering places grouped under it (#55, issue 1).

2026-10-26

  • Changelog entries now live one file per change under changelog/, assembled at build time and released in dated batches with npm run changelog:release, so parallel pull requests no longer conflict on CHANGELOG.md (#53).
  • Those recipes are now stored with each resource and used on resource pages and in level objective totals: Frostbound Valley's 100 Stew needs 67 Veggie (or Meat or Egg) and 7 Sticks instead of "gathered or traded" (#52).
  • Readable recipes for the 32 buildings that said "no single ratio applies": Open Kitchen "3 Stew ← 2 × (Veggie, Meat or Egg) + 1/5 Stick (plates reused)", Winery, Brewery, Cheesery, Trappers' Hut and the rest; planters, storage and the beer stand get plain sentences (#51).
  • Dish pages (Stew, Fondue, Manchamanteles, Barley Porridge) and their eating places say why villagers prefer them over food of the same value: the second effect and eating in company (#50).
  • Needs redesigned: an at-a-glance table of the eight needs, then compact item and place tables per need with meter points and second effects; Houses say crib and doghouse and explain why more houses mean more babies; Gathering places explains the unit; the Bonfire page says how long a stick burns (#49).
  • Level pages list visitors by arrival day ("30 adults on day 50, announced from the start" on Sold Out; three waves on Anthology) and other timed events such as rain and no-births notices; extraction buildings say what they gather and that nothing goes in; the Fishing Dock page states its random catch and real pile size (#48).
  • Every table sorts by clicking a column heading; numbers sort as numbers and blanks stay last (#46).
  • Wiki changelog: this file, rendered at the top of the Changelog page as "Wiki changes".
  • Guides elsewhere: a links page for guides, videos and communities on other sites, a suggestion form, per-level links in Strategy sections and a weekly dead-link check (#43).
  • Level pages: Starting conditions now resolve workplaces, homes, roads, the starting leader, wild trees, growing crops, rock and reed sources, loose piles, tools, salvage and scripted arrivals; the Levels directory gains a "You start with" column. See Morrigwyn Stones (#42).
  • docs/PLAYTESTS.md: step-by-step in-game test protocols for a computer-use agent, separate from the file-decode questions (#44).
  • Gathering places: what one visit to each park, plaza, kitchen or pub gives, read from the decoded social spots; building infoboxes and the Needs page use the same data (#41).
  • Houses: every house compared, with baby and pet spots, cost per villager, heating, biomes and the reproduction values that make spare housing matter (#40).
  • Villagers fed: a Feeds row on plant pages, a Feeds column on the Plants overview and a ranked "How much each source feeds" table on the Food page (#40).
  • Trees: a Spreads row with the stored self-seeding chance; the Fishing Dock states its random catch and the Bonfire the warmth its tender gains (#39).
  • Salvage: level Starting conditions list what ruins, crates, canoes, pallets and gifts return when dismantled, with a total (#30).
  • Needs page: "How eating works", from the stored hunger meter and the villagers' eat priority: the timeline from full to starving, the urge-to-eat table and what each food is worth (#28).
  • Level objective totals round up to whole items, and gathered items (sticks, logs, stone, fish, field crops) stop at the map instead of being worked back through a factory. See Mom's Gardens (#27).
  • Plants: pages show the sandbox biomes that plant each crop or tree, the building that plants it there and where it grows wild; "Planted by" is read from data, so the Nopal Tree is planted by the Oasis (#26).
  • Plants: yields lead by plant type, "orchard trees" are called fruit trees, and fruit trees get a Rate row comparing fruit per day and how fast a full tree fills (#26).
  • Spoilers page: a note on how unreleased status is decided from the files' version gates; construction-cost rollups tracked in issue 23 (#24).
  • Plants become their own section with felling yields and fruit intervals for every tree; work schedules are described against the clock dial (#24).
  • Decoded mechanics on the site: output capacities, heating, baby and pet spots, version gates, need meters, concern effects, Tropical weather and tournament boosts (#22).
  • Resources store their own recipe (made_from), shown per unit on resource pages, and level pages gain "What the objectives need" (#19).

2026-10-25

  • Companion layer: infoboxes, verification badges, uses and required-by links, grouped level availability, leader and story links, navboxes, catalog columns, contribution links and the CI test workflow (#13).
  • Level strategies: a contribution form and a Strategy section on every level page (#14).
  • Version history: per-entity History sections, the Changelog page of game updates, and seeded backdating (#15).
  • Production model, work schedules and ratio corrections on building pages; the information-needed list for the local agent (#16, #17).
  • Publishing from CI to the custom domain; Tournament Mode page and naming; spoilers demoted in navigation; issue forms; need effects stated by building descriptions (#18).
  • Stored-configuration additions: biome per level, starting conditions, the Needs page, age limits, harvest sources and placement rules (#22 groundwork).

2026-10-24

  • Scenario extraction: 133 decoded level pages with exact objectives, day limits, build and plant lists and need flags; objective wording distinguishes Have, Create and HaveInBuilding.

Game updates

Earlier updates are backdated only where an official source or decoded marker ties content to an update. Future builds are compared with scripts/compare-content.mjs, and reviewed differences are added here.
