Maps where no babies are born are now flagged. On 16 maps (including Alike!, An Apple a Day, Full Tilt Queso, Retirement Town, Summer Camp, Overgrown and Mother Knows Best) a map rule switches births off for the whole level; each of those level pages now says so near the top, and the Villagers page lists them. The Births rules on the Villagers page are corrected to match the game code: every house with an empty crib rolls once a game day, and "free housing" means free beds (107).
