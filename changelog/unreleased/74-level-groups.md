Campaigns & levels now splits campaign levels from challenge levels, and groups the challenges by type: Original, Redux and Bounty in the current rotation, and the older Chill, Ranked and Remix sets with their release dates (every two weeks from July 2024 to March 2025). The Challenges page explains the rotation, which repeats every 52 weeks, and has a small calculator that says when a challenge's slot comes round again (74).
