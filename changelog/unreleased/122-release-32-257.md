The Wetlands biome page lists its sandbox build list and map resources, Spookylands gains the nine buildings it got in this update, and every biome page now names the buildings all biomes share (Brick Road and Advanced Warehouse joined them). Roads and walking adds the Wetlands’ shallow marshes, where villagers walk ×0.7, and confirms that Brick and Stone Roads are the same speed.
