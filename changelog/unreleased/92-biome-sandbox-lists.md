Biome pages now have a "Sandbox build list": the crops, trees, houses, workplaces and decoration a new sandbox game in that biome offers, which building plants each crop, what other biomes offer that this one does not, and which hand-made maps in the biome still have those buildings. Spookylands, for example, has no Trading Outpost, Foresters' Hub or Fishing Dock in sandbox, but A Moon Part and two Redux maps on its scene do offer the outpost (92).
