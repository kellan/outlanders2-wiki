Challenges

How the challenge rotation works, the six challenge types, and the challenge and tournament help text recovered from the game. Scoring remains unverified.

Challenges change at 2pm Eastern. Worked out from the game’s 364-day cycle, so check the game if it looks wrong.

The rotation

Each weekend there is a new Original level. On weekdays Bounty and Redux challenges alternate, each running for 24 hours. Chill, Ranked and Remix are older challenge types: they are still playable from the challenge archive but are no longer part of the rotation.

The game files agree. The code names three current challenge types (Bounty, Redux, Original) and three legacy ones (Chill, Leaderboard, Remix; Leaderboard is what the menu shows as Ranked). The Game Center leaderboards are named Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday Challenge plus Weekend Challenge, and the briefings say an Original’s leaderboard runs Friday to Sunday and a Redux’s or Bounty’s for 24 hours. The archive is downloaded from the game’s servers and shows a date on each entry.

Before the rotation, legacy challenges came out in batches of three (one Chill, one Ranked, one Remix) every two weeks, from 3 Jul 2024 to 26 Mar 2025.

Only the legacy levels are in the installed files: 81 maps in 27 batches, of which 20 were released.

Original, Redux and Bounty levels are downloaded when they run. The wiki has all 260 from the first cycle, decoded from the game’s download cache: 52 Original, 104 Redux, 104 Bounty, one per day from 2 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2026. The cycle is 364 days (52 weeks), so every level comes back 364 days later on the same weekday: “Save the Forests” Redux first ran on 28 Oct 2025 and came back on 27 Oct 2026. A day’s challenge starts at 2pm Eastern and runs until 2pm the next day.
