The rotation

Original, Redux and Bounty make up the rotation. An Original starts each Friday and runs through the weekend. On weekdays, Bounty (Mondays and Wednesdays) and Redux (Tuesdays and Thursdays) run for 24 hours each. Challenges change at 18:00 UTC.

The rotation is a 364-day cycle of 260 levels: 52 Original, 104 Redux, 104 Bounty. Each level comes back 364 days after it last ran, on the same weekday. For example, “Save the Forests” Redux first ran on 28 Oct 2025 and came back on 27 Oct 2026. The first cycle ran from 2 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2026; the wiki has every level from it, decoded from the game’s download cache.

Chill, Ranked and Remix are older types, still playable from the archive. Before the rotation, one of each came out every two weeks, from 3 Jul 2024 to 26 Mar 2025. Their maps ship with the game: 60 were released, and 21 more were packaged but never released.

In the game’s code the six types are Original, Redux and Bounty, and Chill, Leaderboard and Remix; Leaderboard is what the menu calls Ranked. Tournament Mode is a separate daily mode with its own levels.
