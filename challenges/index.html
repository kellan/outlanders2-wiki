PLAY MODES

Challenges

Recovered challenge and tournament help, objective templates and candidate maps. Archive categories and representative briefings have been inspected. Live scheduling and scoring remain unverified.

Original, Redux, Bounty, Chill, Ranked and Remix are confirmed archive categories. Tournament Mode is a separate daily, turn-based mode; its text uses the internal prefix “FastMode”.

Six challenge types

All six names were observed in the challenge archive. The examples below were checked against their briefings on 2026-10-24; they are not universal rules for every challenge in a category.

Original: Briefing describes a new complete level, like campaign levels; its leaderboard text says Friday through Sunday.

Redux: Briefing describes a simpler or different version of a previous level; its leaderboard text says 24 hours.

Bounty: Stock objective. Briefing describes producing or holding the target resource and says its leaderboard stays active for 24 hours.

Chill: This Chill example has a time limit.

Ranked: No day limit or optional objective displayed on the briefing. Scoring not tested.

Remix: Archive date displayed: 3/26/2025. This is a displayed archive date, not an independently established first release date.

Archived leaderboard wording does not establish a currently active event. Scoring and completion behavior have not been tested.
