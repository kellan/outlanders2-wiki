Notable Wine Trial is a Bounty challenge. It first ran on Wednesday 3 Dec 2025 and comes back every 364 days.

Stockpile

Wild trees

Salvage
Pallet ×2 (1 Plank each). Dismantling them all gives 2 Plank

Counted from the map file, leaving out 4 scenery objects (signs, birds, statues, story props). Stockpile numbers are items placed on the map and can differ slightly from what the stockpile shows. Wild tree counts are what the map starts with; trees spread and get felled from there.

Main objectives

In 40 days: Produce as many glasses of Wine as you can.

Reach the end of the given time to see how well you did compared to other players!

Objectives are worded as the game shows them: the level’s own objective text where it has one, otherwise the game’s standard objective lines.
