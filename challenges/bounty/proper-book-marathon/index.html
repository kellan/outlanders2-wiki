Counted from the map file, leaving out 24 scenery objects (signs, birds, statues, story props). Stockpile numbers are items placed on the map and can differ slightly from what the stockpile shows. Wild tree counts are what the map starts with; trees spread and get felled from there.

Scoring goal

Have as much Book as you can within the day limit. Your result goes on the challenge leaderboard.

Strategy

No community strategy has been recorded for this level yet. If you have finished it, the form takes a build order, what to watch out for, and the game version; a maintainer turns it into a page section.
