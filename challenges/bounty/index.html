CHALLENGE TYPE

Bounty challenges

Make or hold as much of one resource as you can before time runs out.

In the game’s words

This is a Bounty challenge: produce or have as much of the target resource in the given time to rise to the top! This leaderboard will stay active for 24 hours.

From the level briefing.

When it runs

Mondays and Wednesdays. Each runs for 24 hours, from 2pm Eastern to 2pm the next day. Each level comes back 364 days after it last ran, on the same weekday. See how the rotation works.
