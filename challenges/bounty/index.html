CHALLENGE TYPE

Bounty challenges

Make or hold as much of one resource as you can before time runs out.

What a Bounty challenge is

This is a Bounty challenge: produce or have as much of the target resource in the given time to rise to the top! This leaderboard will stay active for 24 hours.

The game’s own description, shown on the level briefing.

When it runs

On weekdays, alternating with Redux. Each leaderboard runs for 24 hours. Original, Redux and Bounty make up the current rotation; see how the rotation works.

Stock objective. Briefing describes producing or holding the target resource and says its leaderboard stays active for 24 hours.

Levels

Bounty levels are downloaded from the game’s servers when they run. They are not in the installed game files, so the wiki does not have their maps yet. Seen in the archive so far: Mysterious Gingerbread Man Seminar.

Other challenge types: Original · Redux · Chill · Ranked · Remix. All levels, campaigns included, are in one table on All levels.
