CHALLENGE TYPE

Remix challenges

An older challenge type, still playable from the archive. Going by their file names, several are reworked campaign levels.

What a Remix challenge is

An older challenge type, still playable from the archive. Going by their file names, several are reworked campaign levels.

When it runs

No longer in the rotation, but still playable from the challenge archive. Before the rotation began, one Remix level came out every two weeks from 3 Jul 2024 to 26 Mar 2025, released together with one Chill and one Ranked.
