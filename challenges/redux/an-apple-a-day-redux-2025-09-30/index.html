"An Apple a Day" Redux is a Redux challenge, a simpler or different version of An Apple a Day. It first ran on Tuesday 30 Sep 2025 and comes back every 364 days.

Counted from the map file, leaving out 1 scenery object (signs, birds, statues, story props). Stockpile numbers are items placed on the map and can differ slightly from what the stockpile shows. Wild tree counts are what the map starts with; trees spread and get felled from there.

Main objectives

ObjectiveWhat counts
Have 10 Veggies.Stock on hand: have this many at once. Making them earlier is not enough.
Have everyone settled.Every villager has a home.

Optional objectives

None.

Objectives are worded as the game shows them: the level’s own objective text where it has one, otherwise the game’s standard objective lines.

What the objectives need

Items the objectives ask for, plus the ingredients to make them, worked back through each recipe using the buildings this map offers. Amounts are rounded up to whole items; allow extra for anything villagers use along the way. Items that can be picked up or harvested from the map stop there rather than being worked back through a factory.
