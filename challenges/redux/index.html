CHALLENGE TYPE

Redux challenges

Tuesdays and Thursdays, in the current rotation.

In the game’s words

This is a Redux challenge: a simpler or different version of a previous level. This leaderboard will stay active for 24 hours.

From the level briefing.

When it runs

Tuesdays and Thursdays. Each runs for 24 hours, from 18:00 UTC to 18:00 UTC the next day. Each level comes back 364 days after it last ran, on the same weekday. See how the rotation works.
