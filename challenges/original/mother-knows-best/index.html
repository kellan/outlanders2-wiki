Ruins ×2 (10 Stone each), Expedition Crate (DriedMeat) ×2 (3 Plank + 10 Dried Meat each), Ruined Zen Garden (3 Stone), Expedition Crate (Bread) ×3 (3 Plank + 5 Bread each), Ruined Plaza (4 Plank), Expedition Crate (DriedMeat) ×3 (3 Plank + 5 Dried Meat each), Canoe (3 Log), Pallet ×3 (1 Plank each), Ruined House (3 Log). Dismantling them all gives 23 Stone, 31 Plank, 35 Dried Meat, 15 Bread, 6 Log

Counted from the map file, leaving out 230 scenery objects (signs, birds, statues, story props). Stockpile numbers are items placed on the map and can differ slightly from what the stockpile shows. Wild tree counts are what the map starts with; trees spread and get felled from there.
