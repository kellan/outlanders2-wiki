CHALLENGE TYPE

Original challenges

A new, complete level, like the ones in the campaigns.

What an Original challenge is

This is an Original challenge: a new, complete level, just like the ones in the Campaigns. This leaderboard will stay active Friday through Sunday!.

The game’s own description, shown on the level briefing.

When it runs

Every weekend. Its leaderboard runs Friday to Sunday. Original, Redux and Bounty make up the current rotation; see how the rotation works.

Briefing describes a new complete level, like campaign levels; its leaderboard text says Friday through Sunday.

Levels

Original levels are downloaded from the game’s servers when they run. They are not in the installed game files, so the wiki does not have their maps yet. Seen in the archive so far: Counterspell.

Other challenge types: Redux · Bounty · Chill · Ranked · Remix. All levels, campaigns included, are in one table on All levels.
