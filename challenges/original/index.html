CHALLENGE TYPE

Original challenges

Weekends, in the current rotation.

In the game’s words

This is an Original challenge: a new, complete level, just like the ones in the Campaigns. This leaderboard will stay active Friday through Sunday!.

From the level briefing.

When it runs

Every weekend. An Original starts at 2pm Eastern on Friday and stays up until Monday’s Bounty starts at 2pm. Each level comes back 364 days after it last ran, on the same weekday. See how the rotation works.
