CHALLENGE TYPE

Original challenges

A new, complete level, like the ones in the campaigns.

What an Original challenge is

This is an Original challenge: a new, complete level, just like the ones in the Campaigns. This leaderboard will stay active Friday through Sunday!.

The game’s own description, shown on the level briefing.

When it runs

Every weekend. Its leaderboard runs Friday to Sunday. Each Original is dated the Friday it starts. A day’s challenge starts at 2pm Eastern and runs until 2pm the next day. Original, Redux and Bounty make up the current rotation; see how the rotation works.
