CHALLENGE TYPE

Chill challenges

An older challenge type, still playable from the archive. Some have a day limit, as Town of the Year does.

What a Chill challenge is

An older challenge type, still playable from the archive. Some have a day limit, as Town of the Year does.

When it runs

No longer in the rotation, but still playable from the challenge archive. Before the rotation began, one Chill level came out every two weeks from 3 Jul 2024 to 26 Mar 2025, released together with one Ranked and one Remix.
