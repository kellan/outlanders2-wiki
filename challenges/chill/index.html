CHALLENGE TYPE

Chill challenges

An older challenge type. Every released Chill level has a day limit.

When it runs

No longer in the rotation, but still playable from the challenge archive. Before the rotation began, one Chill level came out every two weeks from 3 Jul 2024 to 26 Mar 2025, together with one Ranked and one Remix.
