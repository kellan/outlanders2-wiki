MECHANICS

Villagers and life

Ages, births, and how long a villager lasts when a need runs out, from the stored life-stage and need settings.

Ages

Villagers age in game days, and a game day is five minutes at normal speed, so a baby born today is an adult in about 80 minutes.

StageFrom dayNotes
Baby0Sleeps in the house’s crib; one crib per house
Kid3Leaves the crib
Adult16Works, and counts for births
Elder60Whether elders still work is not stored
Dies of old age75–95Villagers present when a map starts are at most 85 days old

Leaders arrive as adults from age 20 and skip childhood. Each day a villager’s Wellness is met adds 0.2 days to their lifespan. Animals are quicker: cows and sheep live about 7 days, chickens 28–32.

Births

The game checks pairs of adults (2 per check) with a base chance of 14%. With no free housing the chance drops by 97.5%, to almost nothing; happiness adds up to 40%; a met Community need multiplies it by 1.2; and each child per adult already in the village takes off 40%. Population stops at 200.

Every house has exactly one crib, held until the baby turns 3, so the number of houses caps how many babies can exist at once. “Free housing” most likely means a free crib. That fits what players see: building more houses is the surest way to more babies. How often the check runs is in code and not decoded. See Houses.

How villagers die

Four ways: old age, hunger, cold and thirst. Cold and thirst only apply on levels that switch on Heat or Water. The other needs make villagers unhappy but never kill.
