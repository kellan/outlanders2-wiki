PRODUCTION CHAIN

Cheesery

How the Cheesery turns what it takes in into what it makes, step by step. Steps are listed in file order, which is not necessarily the order workers follow.

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

In-game field guide

  • Field guide: 1 Stick → 6 Cheese.
  • Provides needs: Food 3.
  • Construction: 10 Log + 8 Brick. Worker slots: 2.
View field-guide screenshot

Recipe

Per cycle: 1 Stick → 6 Cheese (field guide)

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 36 Cheese (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.

Production notes

From decoded stage timings and a supplied-inputs model; ceilings, not measured output.

  • One cheese wheel is cut into 6 portions of cheese. The material balance of 12 cheese per stick combines two batches; it is not the yield of one wheel.
  • Milking the building’s cows is part of the production process. The model includes the milking action, stirring, unmolding, cutting and fuel preparation. It excludes travel to the cow and equipment changes.
  • Aging takes 400 simulation seconds (1⅓ game days) per wheel, with six aging positions. Fully occupied, those positions have a ceiling of 27 cheese portions per day. More workers do not shorten aging. Cow availability, handoffs and work schedules can leave positions empty.

Ingredient ratio

A complete balance has not been established. Read the individual steps below; alternatives and non-material processes may be involved.
