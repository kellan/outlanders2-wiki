PRODUCTION CHAIN

Bootmakers' Shop

How the Bootmakers' Shop turns what it takes in into what it makes, step by step. Steps are listed in file order, which is not necessarily the order workers follow.

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

In-game field guide

  • Field guide: 2 Animal Hide + 1 Log → 1 Boots.
  • Construction: 12 Plank. Worker slots: 3.
View field-guide screenshot

Recipe

Per cycle: 2 Animal Hide + 1 Log → 1 Boots (field guide)

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 18 Boots and 5 Axe - Iron and 5 Scythe - Iron and 5 Hammer - Iron (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
