PRODUCTION CHAIN

Sawmill

How the Sawmill turns what it takes in into what it makes, step by step. Steps are listed in file order, which is not necessarily the order workers follow.

In-game field guide

  • Field guide: 1 Log → 1 Plank.
  • Construction: 9 Logs. Worker slots: 3.
View field-guide screenshot

Recipe

Ratio: 1 Log → 1 Plank

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 48 Plank (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
