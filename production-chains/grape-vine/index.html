Grape Vine

How the Grape Vine turns what it takes in into what it makes, step by step. Steps are listed in file order, which is not necessarily the order workers follow.

Recipe

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Grape Vine has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.

Ingredient ratio

A complete balance has not been established. Read the individual steps below; alternatives and non-material processes may be involved.
