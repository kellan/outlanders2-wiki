Production

Per cycle: 8 Animal Hide → 9 Wheat or 6 Glass Pane (field guide)

The outpost trades one good native to its biome for one of two goods from other biomes. You choose which one to receive, and only one is active at a time: the building's outputs are exclusive, and the game's hint says “Open the Resources tab to choose the resource you want to receive.”

BiomeGivesReceives (choose one)Levels
Tropiclands8 Glass Pane6 Animal Hide or 9 Wheat73
Evergreen12 Wheat6 Animal Hide or 6 Glass Pane70
Winterlands (this page)8 Animal Hide9 Wheat or 6 Glass Pane93

There is no Drylands outpost in the game files. A base version and a non-recyclable Winterlands copy used only in test levels are left out.

How a trade runs

Workers carry 8 Animal Hide into the outpost's store (it holds 8). Once the store is full, a worker lights a fire; the fire costs nothing. The trade then runs without a worker, and only while the outpost's output store (9) is empty. The goods come out one at a time into a pile of up to 36 for haulers to collect. How long a trade takes is set by an animation, not stored data. One timed Evergreen trade took 216 to 232 seconds at normal speed from a full store to the last good unloaded, about three quarters of a game day, with one worker. A second with three workers took 234 to 269 seconds, so extra workers did not visibly speed it up; they still help carry the input in (see Seen in play).
