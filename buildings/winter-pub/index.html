Outlands Field GuideWorked out from the game files

Pub workers take Wheats and distill them into Wheatshine, a special drink that raises Fun and Heat.

Needs

11

Provides

4→Fun2Heat2

Need values as the field guide shows them. Wheatshine itself restores Fun 4, Heat 4; see Happiness and Gathering places for the rest.

Cost

108

Workers

3

Production

  • 4 Wheatshine ← 1 Stick + 1 Wheat (4 Empty House Drink reused)

Read from the stored steps: fuel and intermediates are folded into the inputs, containers that come back from the eating or drinking spots are marked reused, and “any of” means the step takes whichever is available. Per-cycle speed is not stored.
