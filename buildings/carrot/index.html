A field crop that yields 3 Veggie per full field (3 plants, 1 each). Planted and harvested by the Oasis or Farm. It grows in 9 days (4 stages). In sandbox it can be planted in Drylands and Evergreen.

Biomes

Sandbox biomes that let you plant it, and the building that does the planting there. Story and challenge maps use their own planting lists, shown under “Where it can be planted”.
