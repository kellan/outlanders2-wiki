A field crop that yields 2 Veggie per full field (1 planting spot). Planted and harvested by the Farm. It grows in 7 days (4 stages). In sandbox it can be planted in Evergreen and Spookylands.

Biomes

Sandbox biomes that let you plant it, and the building that does the planting there. Story and challenge maps use their own planting lists, shown under “Where it can be planted”.

BiomePlanted byGrows wild
EvergreenFarmNo
SpookylandsFarmNo

Where it can be planted

Listed in the build or plant list of 2 levels, grouped by campaign and story.
