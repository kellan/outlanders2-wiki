Input-to-output ratio per 1 Meat from the formula graph. It is not a batch size, a storage capacity or a production rate. The whole-number form is 8 Stick + 5 Meat + 5 Mushroom → 40 Stroganoff.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Stroganoff Cookhouse has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
