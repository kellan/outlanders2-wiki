An advanced warehouse where its workers gather Construction Materials such as Clay, Planks and Bricks.

Construction inputs

4 Plank

Production

Open full chain →

Takes in Brick, Clay and Plank for storage; nothing is produced.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 216 (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
