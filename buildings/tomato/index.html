A field crop that yields 3 Veggie per full field (one plant). Planted and harvested by the Farm. It grows in 10 days (4 stages). In sandbox it can be planted in Evergreen and Tropiclands.

Biomes

Sandbox biomes that let you plant it, and the building that does the planting there. Story and challenge maps use their own planting lists, shown under “Where it can be planted”.
