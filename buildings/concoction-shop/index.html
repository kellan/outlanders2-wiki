Outlands Field GuideWorked out from the game files

Concoction brewers make pots out of Clay and brew Concoctions in them using Mushrooms they pick from the ground on a fire fueled by Sticks.

111

1→Wellness2

Need values as the field guide shows them. Concoction itself restores Wellness 4; see Happiness and Gathering places for the rest.

Ingredient ratio per 1 Clay, worked out from the production steps. It is not a batch size, a storage limit or a speed. The whole-number form is 3 Stick + 5 Mushroom + 15 Clay → 15 Concoction.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 12 Concoction (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
