Canteen makers take Glass Panes and Reed Bunches, and heat them in a fire fueled by Sticks to create an equippable Canteen that raises the owner's base Cooling, making it easier to stay cool.

Ingredient ratio, worked out from the production steps. It is not a batch size, a storage limit or a speed.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Canteen Factory has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
