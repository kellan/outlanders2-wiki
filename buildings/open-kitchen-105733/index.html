Outlands Field GuideChecked against the in-game field guide

Open Kitchen cooks take any combination of two Veggies, Meats or Eggs and cook them over a fire fueled by Sticks. The result is a delicious Stew that provides Food, while the social environment raises Community.

Why villagers come here: eating Stew here gives Community 4 on top of the food, and eating in company ranks a little above eating alone, so villagers prefer this over food of the same value elsewhere. See Gathering places and how eating works.

Production

Open full chain →
  • 3 Stew ← 2 × (Veggie, Meat or Egg) + 1/5 Stick (3 Plate reused)

Read from the stored steps: fuel and intermediates are folded into the inputs, containers that come back from the eating or drinking spots are marked reused, and “any of” means the step takes whichever is available. Per-cycle speed is not stored.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Open Kitchen has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
