A forestry tree that yields 2 Log per tree when felled (3 chops). Planted by the Foresters' Hub and harvested by the Foresters' Hub or Lumberjacks' Base. It grows in 8 days (4 stages). In sandbox it can be planted in Drylands.

Biomes

Sandbox biomes that let you plant it, and the building that does the planting there. Story and challenge maps use their own planting lists, shown under “Where it can be planted”.
