Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Fondue makers melt Cheese over a fire fueled by Sticks and add chopped Veggies to create a pot of Fondue that provides Food and Fun.

Why villagers come here: eating Fondue here gives Fun 4 on top of the food, and eating in company ranks a little above eating alone, so villagers prefer this over food of the same value elsewhere. See Gathering places and how eating works.

Construction inputs

8 Brick10 Plank

Production

Open full chain →
  • 5 Fondue ← 1 Cheese + 1 Stick + 2 Veggie (5 Fondue Empty Plate reused)

Read from the stored steps: fuel and intermediates are folded into the inputs, containers that come back from the eating or drinking spots are marked reused, and “any of” means the step takes whichever is available. Per-cycle speed is not stored.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Fondue Bar has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
