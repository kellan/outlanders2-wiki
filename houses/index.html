How the spots work

A house's villager spots are its sleeping places; the game counts a house as full when they are taken. Each house also carries a one-spot baby house that accepts only babies, so a newborn does not take a villager spot. Each also carries a one-spot pet house. The files say nothing about what lives there beyond the name; we have not seen a pet in play, so treat it as unconfirmed. Whether kids and elders count against the same spots as adults, and whether a family can split across houses, has not been tested.

Why spare houses matter

Births are checked against the stored reproduction values: a base chance of 14% per check for every 2 adults, raised by happiness (×0.4 of it) and lowered by 40% per child already per adult. With no free housing the chance is multiplied by 0.025, so a settlement with full houses all but stops having babies. Population is capped at 200. How often the check runs is not stored.
