Stored settings are what the game files say. Nobody has timed most of them in play, so a page states a stored number as stored and says when its meaning is a reading of the data rather than a measurement.

Not written yet

These need data the files have not given up yet. The full list, with the test that would settle each one, is in INFORMATION-NEEDED.

  • Trading. Each biome’s Trading Outpost trades one local good for your choice of two others (Evergreen: 12 Wheat for 6 Glass Pane or 6 Animal Hide), and the files hold no other trader. One timed trade took about three quarters of a game day with one worker; whether more workers or other biomes change that is untested.
  • Roads and walking. Base walking speed and the road bonus are not in the exported data. What is stored: Boots walk ×1.4 (×1.2 on roads) and a villager whose Fun is met walks ×1.2.
  • Desert and winter penalties. The Heat, Cooling and Water meters are decoded; whether Desert or Winter maps change how fast they fall, beyond the Tropical weather, is not.
  • Leader abilities. Leader effects are not stored with the leaders; pages show only their descriptions.
