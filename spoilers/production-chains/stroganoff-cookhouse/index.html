PRODUCTION CHAIN

Stroganoff Cookhouse

How the Stroganoff Cookhouse turns what it takes in into what it makes, step by step.

Game data v30 · build 254Sources & research notes

Recipe

Ratio: 1.6 Stick + 1 Meat + 1 Mushroom → 8 Stroganoff

Each Stroganoff: 1 Stick for every 5 Stroganoff, 1 Meat for every 8 Stroganoff, 1 Mushroom for every 8 Stroganoff

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 20 Stroganoff (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
