NEED

Heat

Warmth, only on maps that switch it on (the Winterlands). It kills.

All mechanicsNeedsHow eating worksHappinessVillagers and lifeHeat, cooling and waterDays and timeGearHousesGathering placesSandbox

All needs · Food · Hydration · Energy (sleep) · Fun · Cooling · Wellness · Community

Extreme low temperatures in the Winterlands make survival tough. Being cold for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

With nothing restoring it, a villager lasts about 7.5 days (38 minutes) from the start of a level. One unit restores 25 points against a fall of 20 a day, so about 0.8 units a day keep it level. Stored settings; not yet timed in play.
