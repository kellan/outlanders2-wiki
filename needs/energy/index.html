One unit restores 8 points against a fall of 20 a day, so about 2.5 units a day keep it level. Stored settings; not yet timed in play.

Sleep

Restored by sleeping in a house. No item carries units for it; a villager with nowhere to sleep rests where they stand. See Houses.
