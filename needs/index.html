MECHANICS

Needs

What each villager need is satisfied by, from the stored resource configuration. How fast needs fall, what gear and buildings do, and how the Boost works are not yet established.

Hydration

The conditions of the Drylands are a challenge for outlanders. Being dehydrated for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Nopalito4 unitsWater1 unit

Enabled by stored setting on 4 of 130 levels. Absent settings in older maps are not treated as disabled.

Heat

Extreme low temperatures in the Winterlands make survival tough. Being cold for too long will result in death. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Wheatshine adults only4 units

Enabled by stored setting on 39 of 130 levels. Absent settings in older maps are not treated as disabled.

Energy (sleep)

Restored by sleeping. No resource carries units for it.

Cooling

High temperatures in the Tropiclands make daytime work tough. Lack of Cooling will reduce a villager's movement speed. Satisfying this need activates the Boost, making it easier to satisfy every other need.

Housing

Housing is a need switch on levels but has no resource; it is met by houses. See the Buildings catalog, Housing tab, for capacities.
