The eight needs

Every need is a meter. It falls by a set amount each day and is topped up by eating or drinking something, visiting a place, wearing gear or sleeping. Below its low warning villagers complain; above its met line it counts towards happiness. Three needs kill when they run out: Food at −100, Heat and Hydration at −50. Each need has its own page with its meter, the items and places that restore it, and the levels that switch it on.

Meter points = units × what one unit restores for that need. A 2-unit Bread is 120 points on a Food meter that falls 40 a day; a 1.2-unit plaza visit is 30 points on a Community meter that falls 20 a day. These are the stored settings; nobody has timed them in play.
