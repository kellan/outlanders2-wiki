Seen in play

  • One fisher at one Fishing Dock position caught 0, 1 or 2 Meat per cast: over 20 casts, four 0s, five 1s and eleven 2s, 27 Meat in all (1.35 a cast). A 2 can only come from the stored 0–2 spot, so this is that spot; the two 0–1 spots were not tested. tested in play, 2026-10-27

  • A Fishing Dock stops at 72 Meat: its stock reached 72 and the dock showed "No more storage space" with three fishers assigned. This matches the stored pile capacity of 72. tested in play, 2026-10-27

    Evergreen sandbox, day 41; Joshua, Violet and Nancy assigned, nothing taking Meat away until after the observation. The agent also found that a lone fisher moves to the first fishing position after the first worker is removed, so removing workers does not isolate the other positions.

Production

Open full chain →

Produces Meat straight from the map; no materials go in.

3 work spots. Each cycle lands a random catch: 0–1 at 2 spots, 0–2 at 1 spot, so about 0.67 per cycle per spot on average, and catches come unevenly by design. (stored on the workspot; not timed in play)

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 72 Meat (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
