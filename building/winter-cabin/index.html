This house features a wood stove that burns Sticks to raise its occupants' Heat.

Provides

4 villagers sleep here

Cost

33

Construction inputs

3 Stone3 Plank

Production

Open full chain →

A keeper feeds Stick into the fire; each one burns for a day, and the fire holds 2, so it stays lit about 2 days with nobody tending it. It warms 4 sleeping spots. The keeper does not need to stay once it is lit.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. The output capacity of the Winter Cabin has not been established. Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
