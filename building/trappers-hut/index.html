Seen in play

  • Trappers' Huts hunt rabbits from Winter Pines planted by a Foresters' Hub, not only from wild trees. player report, 2026-10-26

    The game files agree: rabbit burrows are spawned by the Winter Pine itself (a Burrow Spawner on the tree prefab: at most 1 burrow per pine, a new one every 6 to 10 days). Because the spawner is part of the tree, a planted pine grows burrows the same way a wild one does. Each rabbit gives 2 Meat and 1 Animal Hide (Trappers' Hut field guide).

Production

Open full chain →
  • 2 Meat + 1 Animal Hide ← 1 HunterTrap (full) brought back by the workers

Read from the stored steps: fuel and intermediates are folded into the inputs, containers that come back from the eating or drinking spots are marked reused, and “any of” means the step takes whichever is available. Per-cycle speed is not stored.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 36 Meat and 18 Animal Hide (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
