Outlands Field GuideWorked out from the game files

Gingerbread Man makers take Flour and Eggs and cook them in an oven fueled by Sticks to create Gingerbread Men that raise both Fun and Food.

Ingredient ratio, worked out from the production steps. It is not a batch size, a storage limit or a speed.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 40 Gingerbread Man (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
