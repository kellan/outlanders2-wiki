Outlands Field GuideWorked out from the game files

Glass workers light a fire with Sticks and, through the use of science, make Glass Panes out of common sand. You read that right.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 48 Glass Pane (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
