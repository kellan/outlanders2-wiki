Outlands Field GuideChecked against the in-game field guide

Cookie makers take Flour and Eggs and cook them in an oven fueled by Sticks to create funny-shaped Cookies that raise both Fun and Food.

Ingredient ratio, worked out from the production steps. It is not a batch size, a storage limit or a speed.

How production works

Workers repeat the recipe while inputs are available and stop when the building's output store is full. Its output store holds 40 Cookie (decoded pile sizes). Capacity and recipe say nothing about speed: cycle time is not yet measured for any building.
