Christmas

Everything Christmas-themed in Outlanders 2: the Gingerbread Man Shop and its Gingerbread Men, the giant Gingerbread Man Man, and every map decorated with presents, candy canes and lights.

Buildings

NameWhat it is
Gingerbread Man ManOnly in Christmas form
Gingerbread Man ShopOnly in Christmas form

Presents

The wrapped presents placed on Christmas maps are worth opening: recycle one and it gives 3 Coat or 1 Coat + 2 Book, depending on the present. Each Christmas level page lists its presents under Salvage, with the total.
