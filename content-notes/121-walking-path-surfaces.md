# Roads and walking: paths only in the surface table

The decoded `walking.surfaces` list includes building footprints (Warehouse, Advanced Warehouse, Fitness Park, Communal Plaza, Zen Garden, Playground) at ×1.2 alongside Roads and the Bridge. That is accurate, but a reader choosing what to lay down for travel only places roads and bridges, so the table keeps those (names matching Road or Bridge) and the building grounds are listed in a note with their multiplier. The data is unchanged.

The "When to build a road" section (rules of thumb, road cost and payback, trip examples) moved from the top of the page to the end, after the speed and surface tables and "Other things that change speed", so the advice follows the numbers it relies on (Boots, the Fun bonus).
