# Coypus page

Maintainer request (22 November 2026): "Add a page on Coypus". New Mechanics page at `coypus/`, built from a new `trapping` record in `decoded-mechanics.json`.

**Source.** Research `tools/export_trapping.py` on the version 32 (257) decode, output `data/trapping-32-257.json`. It reads the burrow spawners on trees (`SpawnerBehaviour` whose prefab is a burrow), the burrow prefabs (`LocationConstraints`, `WorkspotRandomTimeInRangeCondition`, `DestroyWhenWorkspotFinishedAndEmpty`, `RemoteWorkspot`) and the trapper huts (`WorkplaceConfig.AreaOfEffect` bitmap, the Trap -> Meat & Hide formula, trap piles). Rabbits (Winter Pine, Trappers' Hut) come out with the same timings and area; only coypus get a page.

**What the page states, and why:**

- **Burrows:** only `CypressTree/Otter Spawner` spawns `CoypuBurrow` (also research `docs/release-32-257/buildings.md`). MaxSpawned 1, every 6–10 days, InitializationTimeOffset −8. From `SpawnerBehaviour.Spawn` and `SpawnerSystem` (ported in research `tools/villageport/spawner.py`): when a roll comes due and the burrow is still there, nothing spawns and the next roll counts from then. That gives "the tree waits another 6–10 days". The first roll counts from 8 days before the start, so a delay under 8 (half of a uniform 6–10) is already due at load: "about half the trees already have a burrow, the rest within 2 days". Valid terrains [2, 3] are sand and soil.
- **Trapping:** the burrow's workspot turns an empty trap into a full one at a random time between 0.75 and 0.95 of the day, with `RequireWorker` false ("nobody needs to be there"; late on the night face, per the Days and time page). `CompleteAmountBeforeDestroy` 1 with `DestroyWhenWorkspotFinishedAndEmpty`: one catch, then the burrow goes. The hut turns 1 full trap into 2 Meat + 1 Animal Hide + the empty trap. The page leaves out how many traps a hut holds (trap piles of 6 and 2): how many exist in play isn't settled.
- **Reach:** `WorkplaceBehaviour.UpdateAvailableWorkspots` only adds remote work spots whose placeable is inside the area of effect (ported in `tools/villageport/workplace.py`). One bitmap cell is a 2×2 block of grid units, one square on screen (`GetDefaultAreaOfEffectEnumerator`); the 14×14 bitmap has the hut's own 2×2 as its hole, so it reaches 6 squares out from each side, with rounded corners. The same mapping gives the Farm's 18×18 tiles in research `docs/strategy-research/port-pilot.md`.
- **How many trees:** one coypu per tree per mean roll gap (8 days), at most; the table is that rate times 2 Meat and 1 Hide. Real output is lower when burrows wait for trappers.
- **Felling:** the Cypress Tree's `SpawnedWorkspotNotExecutingCondition` (already on the plant record). Both the Lumberjacks' Base and the Foresters' Hub harvest Cypress Trees (the plant page's "Harvested by").
- **Levels:** computed from the scenario records: Cypress Trees at start, whether the hut is in the build list, and Animal Hides the goals need through the build list's recipes (Book Workshop 1 Hide per Book, Bootmakers' Shop 2 per Boots). Clause has 122 Cypress Trees but no hut. On Copies no other building in the build list makes Animal Hide, so the tip divides the 100 Hides by about 8 coypus per tree over 60 days (0.5 at the start + 60 ÷ 8): 13 trees.

Not on the page: rabbits (no page asked for), trap count, and the hut's placement highlight (it highlights other Swamp Trappers' Huts and their build sites).

**The real animal** (maintainer, 22 November: "You can source info from the Wikipedia page on them"). A short section at the end, paraphrased from the English Wikipedia article [Coypu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coypu) as read on 22 November 2026 (CC BY-SA 4.0, credited on the page): size (4–9 kg, 40–60 cm body), burrows in banks, webbed hind feet, diet including willow bark in winter, most active at dusk and around midnight, orange incisors from iron in the enamel, fur farming since the late 1800s, invasive spread, Louisiana's harvest incentives and nutria meat likened to rabbit, and "nutria" meaning otter in Spanish. The game tie-ins (night-time catches, Willow Trees in the Wetlands, the spawner's internal name "Otter Spawner") are from the decoded data above. en.wikipedia.org was added to the session environment's allowed hosts for this.
