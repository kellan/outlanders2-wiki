## Stray hedges and raw data on reader pages (2026-10-28)

After #84 the maintainer asked whether other stray language like "Starting state not checked in play" was scattered across the wiki. A scan of every reader page (not the research pages) for hedging phrases found these, now fixed:

- **Raw JSON on reader pages:** the Challenges, Tournament and three campaign pages showed a "View source evidence" box with a raw data dump, such as `"schedules_and_current_availability_unverified"`. Reader pages that have no research companion now drop these boxes. The About page's artwork note is a plain paragraph.
- **Chain page boilerplate:**
  - Every chain page said "cycle time is not yet measured for any building". That now appears once, on the Production chains index.
  - 91 pages said "A complete balance has not been established".
  - About 50 pages said "The output capacity of X has not been established".
  - The last two are gone. The section appears only when there is something to show.
- **Building pages:** the "Other versions" list no longer says "which one a level uses is not known". The tree regrowth note drops its parenthetical hedge.
- **Spoilers:**
  - Reasons are rewritten in plain words, such as "Used only in Oona's levels. The game files mark it for version 32; the current game is version 30."
  - The banner reads "Spoiler warning".
  - The page intro drops "reported-unreleased … release is unconfirmed".
  - Tournament seasons not yet seen are tagged "Not seen in game yet".

**Kept:** specific open questions that tell the reader something real. Examples: whether kids count against house spots, how the day limit counts, the 2pm rollover after the clocks change, and the Basic House refund rule. The About page keeps its technical limits.
