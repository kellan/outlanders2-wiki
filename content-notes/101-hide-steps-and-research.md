## Production-step and plant-chain pages retired; research pages noindex (2026-11-06)

First step of `docs/EDITORIAL-CLEANUP-PLAN.md`.

- **Production steps.** The 184 unnamed resources (`publicly_named: false`) are internal stages of a building's recipe. Players never see them in game, so they no longer get a reader page, an A–Z entry, a search entry or a sitemap entry, and the resources catalog loses its "Include intermediates" tab. Each old address redirects to the building that makes the step (or uses it, when nothing makes it), choosing the building available on the most levels when several qualify; gear items go to the Gear page; anything else goes to the Resources catalog. The records stay in `content/resources/` and their research pages stay.
- **Plant production chains.** The 64 chain pages for plants (half of them `field-` duplicates) held only an unordered step dump. They redirect to the plant page.
- **Research pages.** The `/verification/` pages carry `noindex, follow` and are left out of `sitemap.xml`. They were 905 of 1,857 sitemap URLs. They remain public, linked from each article, and were already out of site search and `llms.txt`.
- **Same-name copies.** 15 buildings and 24 food or resource records share a name with another record (Halloween versions, "old" definitions, non-recyclable `zNR` roads, 21 Fondue records, FakeStick, EggSpawned). Each name now has one page. The plain address goes to the version listed on the most levels: this swaps Fountain, Playground and Two-Story House away from their Halloween versions and Brick Road to the recyclable one. The Orchard's plain page stays on its "old" definition (90 levels against 52). Building pages end with an Other versions table: a plain label taken from the internal name (Halloween, Older version, Cannot be recycled, Standard), the cost and the level count. Level lists and level counts include every version. The three biome Trading Outposts keep separate pages because each one trades different goods. Every numbered address, old or new, redirects. The research pages for each version stay.
- **Unnamed maps.** The 19 "Unnamed scenario" maps were packaged but never scheduled, so players cannot play them. They no longer have level pages and are dropped from "Where it can be built" lists, biome lists, the All levels table, the home page count and challenge-level previous/next links. They stay as unlinked "Unnamed map" rows in the collapsed "packaged but never released" table on the Chill, Ranked and Remix pages, and their research pages stay. Old addresses go to that challenge type page (or to All levels for the one additional map).
