## Starts on the map (2026-11-07)

Building pages only listed the levels whose starting build list includes the building ("Where it can be built"). The maintainer noticed the Author's Stand page left out "Where Am I?". That level does not list the stand: its build site is already on the map.

**Changes:** every building and plant page now has up to two more sections:
- **Starts on the map:** levels whose initial instances include the building or its build site (`construction.build_site_gameobject_id`), grouped like the other level lists. The lead line counts the levels where it is not also on the build list, so no more can be built there. Examples: Greenhouse 21 levels, 4 without it on the build list; Basic House 109 and 31.
- **Asked for but not offered at the start:** levels whose objectives (other than Have-in-building) name the building, while it is on neither the build list nor the map. It is presumably unlocked during play. Examples: Luthierie in "Secret Oasis" Redux, Swimming Pier in "Dandelion Springs" Redux, Giant Wheel in Wheel of Thirst.

These use the same exact definition as "Where it can be built"; same-name variants keep their own pages. How unlocks during play work is not decoded.
