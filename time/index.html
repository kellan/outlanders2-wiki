Days and time

How long a game day is, how the clock dial reads, when crews work, and what a level’s day limit means in real time.

A game day

A game day is 300 simulation seconds: five minutes at normal speed. Fast mode runs on a separate schedule whose speed is not decoded. The game shows no hours. Its dial shows a day face for the first half of the day and a night face for the second.

Work shifts

Most workplaces start at 0.9 of the day, late in the night face, and stop at 0.5, when the dial turns to night. That is 60% of the day, about 3 minutes. The rest is free time and sleep. The Clay Pit starts and finishes 30 seconds later, which is why its crew looks like late risers. Building pages give each workplace’s shift.

Seen in play

  • The day counter goes up at dawn, as the dial's sun appears, not at sunset or midnight. The night face still belongs to the day before. tested in play, 2026-10-26

    Screenshots bracket the change by about 10 to 15 real seconds. This places the visible day boundary; it does not by itself say which stored shift fraction is dawn. The decoded dial shows its day face for the first half of each day, so a day starting at dawn fits.
